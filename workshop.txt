title=Relentless Dead
description=[h1]RELENTLESS DEAD[/h1]
description=[b]The dead don't stay down.[/b]
description=
description=Killed zombies can get back up where they fell, hours later. Burn them or finish them off to make sure they stay down.
description=
description=[h2]THE DEAD GET BACK UP[/h2]
description=It works as soon as it's enabled, no setup needed. Out of the box half of the zombies you kill get back up an hour or two later, once each. They rise in the clothes they died in, as ordinary zombies, and every rise counts as another kill.
description=
description=[h2]KEEP THEM DOWN[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Burn the corpse with petrol and a lighter.
description=[*]Finish off: right-click a zombie corpse to make sure of it. Each corpse can be finished off once, by anyone, and it's offered on every corpse, so the menu never tells you which ones would rise.
description=[*]Optional: a melee hit to the head of a zombie on the floor keeps it down.
description=[/list]
description=Bandits never rise, nor do zombies that lost their head.
description=
description=[h2]YOUR RULES[/h2]
description=Choose how many rise, how soon, how often one body can rise, whether players' zombies and corpses in safehouses rise, and whether Finish off and head hits are allowed. Everything is a sandbox option, on any server.
description=
description=[h2]OTHER MODS[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Zombie Dismemberment: headless corpses and zombies with no arms and a lost leg stay down, and Finish off takes the head off.
description=[*]Day and night mods can decide when the dead rise: Crepuscular makes the night's kills rise in the dark.
description=[*]Modders can keep their monsters down, keep a corpse down or react to a rise through a small API.
description=[*]Undying Zombies: use one of the two, not both.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]GOOD TO KNOW[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Single player and multiplayer.
description=[*]Admins get a Relentless Dead status entry in the right-click menu.
description=[/list]
